Phone layout up to 729px with a floating glass tab bar (Budget, Transactions, Accounts, Reports, More), a floating Add button on the budget and transaction lists, large page titles under round header buttons, a More sheet, rounded bottom-sheet dialogs, toasts above the bar, visible keyboard focus, pinch zoom and theme colours that keep text at 4.5:1 contrast.
Phone budget with each category group as a rounded card under its Budgeted and Balance totals, rows showing Budgeted and a Balance pill coloured by state (positive, warning, overspent, zero), a To budget card with Move (or Cover when overbudgeted), "Categories overspent" and "Uncategorized" banners, and a category sheet with Balance, Budgeted and Spent, a spent bar, Cover, Move money and the four latest transactions.
Phone transactions with a Transactions tab listing every account under an "Uncategorized" banner, rows showing a category or Uncategorized chip, the bank's payee text and (on that tab) the account, an Upcoming section with status badges, signed day totals and a labelled cleared icon; the transaction editor is a bottom sheet over the previous screen with a purple header (Cancel, title, Delete in the ⋯ menu, amount), Expense, Income and Transfer (Transfer lists accounts only), fields grouped in two cards, recent payee chips, a full-width "Add transaction" button with "Save + new" beside it, split saving blocked until fully assigned, and a sectioned payee picker.
Phone manage screens with accounts as On budget, Off budget and Closed cards under their totals, a purple icon for card accounts, a "New transactions" dot and Synced and Manual pills under each name, card surfaces on schedules, payees, rules and settings, fixed-size report summary numbers, an overdue schedules card with Mark as paid and Skip, a payee A-Z index with duplicate merge suggestions, rules written as sentences and a segmented theme control in Settings.
Tablet layout from 730 to 1099px: the phone pages beside an inset glass rail (Budget, Transactions, Accounts, Reports, More, and a round Add button at its foot), and the budget with Budgeted, Spent and Balance per category and the selected row's details in a side panel.
Desktop with the redesigned sidebar on by default as an inset card, the budget with each category group as a rounded card, Budgeted as an outlined field and Balance as a pill coloured by state, an "Overspent" card with Fix beside To budget, and the selected category's details (Balance, Budgeted and Spent, spent bar, Cover, Move money and recent transactions) in a side panel; desktop register with an Uncleared chip, a bulk toolbar (count, total, Categorize, Mark cleared, Delete) and Upcoming and Posted headings.
